About The Project

Securing Jharkhand's Academic Future

Developed specifically for the SIH 2025 Problem Statement 25029.

The Challenge

With increasing digitization, the problem of fake degrees and forged academic certificates has become a major concern. Cases of fraudulent documents being used for jobs, admissions, or government schemes have highlighted the absence of a robust mechanism.

Veri-Scholars addresses this by providing a unified, AI-driven platform for the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Jharkhand, to validate credentials securely and at scale.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Glassmorphism), Vanilla JS
  • Backend: Python, Flask REST API
  • AI & OCR: Tesseract (pytesseract), OpenCV (cv2)
  • NLP: SentenceTransformers (Entity matching)
  • Database: SQLite with SQLAlchemy ORM
  • Security: SHA-256 Hashing
